# Aleph name and branding

The Apache-2.0 license applies to Aleph-owned source code. It does not license the
Aleph name, logo, mascot or official branding. Branding assets remain outside that
source-code license even when they are present in this repository.

Forks and derivatives may describe their origin factually, for example:
"A fork of Aleph" or "Derived from Aleph." Such descriptions must not imply that
the fork is an official Aleph release or is endorsed by Aleph.

Use distinct names and branding for independently distributed forks. Do not present
Aleph's official branding as the identity of an unofficial release. This policy
does not restrict reasonable factual attribution, or uses permitted by applicable
law. Apache-2.0 section 6 also addresses use of the licensor's names and marks.

No trademark registration or registered status is asserted by this document.
Third-party names and marks remain subject to their respective owners' policies.
