# The Workroom (La Sala) {#sala}

The Workroom is where you **use** your agent. You give it a task and watch it
work, step by step, while the artifact takes shape. The work is what matters, not
the chat.

## What it can do from here (and what it can't) {#alcance}

From the Workroom, the agent works with **what is in plain sight**, and it has all
of it from the first message —nothing to pick, nothing to install—: it **runs
Python code**, reads and writes the conversation's **files**, queries **data** in a
local database, **opens documents** (PDF, Word, spreadsheets) and **browses the
web**. **It does not touch your accounts.** For it to act on an account
—send an email, post to Slack, move money— you first equip or connect that tool
(Gmail, Slack, your bank) in the Room; only then does the agent regain that
permission. If you ask for something it can't do yet, it can **leave it drafted**
for you to send yourself.

## See every step · the gate to the world {#pasos-gate}

You see every step as it happens. And to **send, spend or delete**, it stops and
asks for your OK. That can't be auto-approved in any mode.

## Autonomy {#autonomia}

- **Balanced:** it works on its own locally; it checks with you before touching
  the world.
- **Hard floor:** money, sends and writes to the world **always** ask — in both
  modes. There is no way to turn that floor off.

## Effort {#esfuerzo}

Your CLI applies the real effort and the run records it. **The Workroom has no
effort dial**: there is nothing to choose here.

## Attaching {#adjuntar}

Use the clip in the composer to attach **images and documents** (PDF, Word,
spreadsheets, text, CSV): they stay visible before you send, and you can remove
one if you change your mind. Write what you want done with them and send.

What you attach **stays in the conversation**: two turns later you can still ask
about the same PDF without uploading it again. They are yours and that
conversation's — nobody else sees them.

Documents are always read. **Images depend on the brain you picked**: if it can't
see, the Workroom tells you when you attach, before you type.

## Works that come from a method {#metodo}

If a work comes from a method, its **canonical version is not overwritten**: when
you edit it your change becomes a **separate copy**, and the method itself is
changed from its workflow. If a work came from a method, **the Workroom says so
above the work**, with a link to open it. **Starting a method from here isn't
possible yet** — for now you begin from the method's own screen.

## When a tool-belt fails to load {#cinturon}

If the declared belt fails to load, its tools are **unavailable** until you fix
that recipe or connection. The Workroom tells you instead of pretending the tool
is ready. (See `piezas.en.md` and `conectar.en.md`.)
